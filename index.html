    About

    Curiosity, structured.

    A short introduction to who I am, what pulled me toward databases, and where I'm headed next.

    I'm a Computer Science / Database Administration student who got hooked on the idea that almost every meaningful product is, underneath, a well-organized (or badly organized) database. That realization sent me down a path of learning SQL deeply, studying schema design, and building small backend systems that keep data honest and fast to retrieve.

    Outside of coursework, I read research papers on query optimization, contribute to study groups, and rebuild the same small apps in different stacks just to understand the trade-offs. I care about doing the unglamorous parts well — indexing, normalization, backups — because that's usually where real systems succeed or fail.

    My near-term goal is a data engineering or database administration role where I can keep learning from production systems at scale; my longer-term goal is to design data infrastructure that other engineers can trust without thinking about it.
